No Liability for Self-Organized Activities

The Litter Prevention Network is not responsible for any injuries, accidents, damages, or losses that occur during self-organized cleanup activities or any activities undertaken independently by individuals or groups. All such activities are conducted at the participant's own risk.

Safety First

We strongly encourage all community members to prioritize personal safety at all times. When picking up litter or participating in any outdoor activity:

  • Avoid handling hazardous materials, chemicals, or medical waste — report them to the appropriate authority
  • Never stop in traffic or enter a roadway to retrieve litter
  • Stay away from unsafe areas, unstable terrain, or private property
  • Children must always be supervised by a responsible adult
  • Wear appropriate protective gear including gloves and closed-toe shoes

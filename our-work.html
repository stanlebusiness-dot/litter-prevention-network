What We Do

Hands-on cleanup, every week.

LPN Willis organizes regular neighborhood cleanups, partners with local schools and businesses, and runs a reward program that pays residents for picking up litter. We believe cleanliness starts with community action — not just awareness.

From scheduled cleanup events at local parks to on-demand reporting through our app, every piece of the LPN program is designed to make participation easy and impactful.

🗓️

Scheduled Cleanups

Monthly and seasonal cleanup events at parks, roadsides, and community hotspots throughout Willis. Open to all ages — supplies provided.

📍

Litter Reporting

Residents use our litter map to flag dumping sites and hazardous waste. Reports are reviewed and routed to the right cleanup teams.

🏆

Reward Program

Participants earn points for every verified cleanup. Points are redeemable for gift cards, merch, and local business rewards.

🏫

School Partnerships

We partner with Willis-area schools to bring environmental education and organized cleanups into classrooms and campuses.

🤝

Business Sponsorships

Local businesses fund cleanup events and supplies in exchange for community recognition. Sponsorships keep the program free for everyone.

🚨

Emergency Response

When illegal dumping or hazardous materials are reported, we coordinate rapid response with local authorities and cleanup crews.

Program Timeline

How we've grown

2024 — Founded

LPN Willis launches

Founded by a small group of Willis residents tired of seeing roadside litter go unaddressed. First cleanup event drew 12 volunteers.

2025 — Expanded

Rewards program & digital tools

Launched the points-based reward system and litter reporting map. Partnered with three local businesses as founding sponsors.

2026 — Now

School programs & emergency response

Expanded into Willis-area schools and built out the emergency litter response program for illegal dumping and hazardous materials.

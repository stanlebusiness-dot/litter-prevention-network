What We DoLo Que Hacemos

LPN Willis is a prevention-first community network — educating neighbors and spreading the habits that stop litter before it starts.LPN Willis es una red comunitaria enfocada en la prevención — educando a los vecinos y difundiendo los hábitos que detienen la basura antes de que empiece.

Our Focus

Prevention, not just cleanup

Most litter programs start after the damage is done. LPN Willis starts earlier — building the community habits, awareness, and connections that prevent litter from hitting the ground in the first place. Here's what we're doing right now.

🌿

Prevention Education

Teaching Willis residents the everyday habits that stop litter at the source — securing loads, covering bins, modeling the right behavior at home and on the road.

📢

Community Awareness

Spreading the word through social media, neighbor outreach, and community channels — making prevention a normal part of how Willis residents think and act.

🤝

Neighbor Connection

Building a local network of Willis residents who care about their community — so prevention becomes a shared standard, not a solo effort.

🏫

School Outreach

Working with Willis-area schools to bring prevention education into the classroom and build the habits that stick for life.

🏢

Business Partnerships

Partnering with local Willis businesses to support the mission — spreading prevention messaging and keeping our community resources free for everyone.

🌐

Bilingual Outreach

All of our content and outreach is available in English and Spanish — because a cleaner Willis means reaching every neighbor in our community.

Our Story

How we got here

2024 — Founded

LPN Willis launches

Founded by Willis residents tired of seeing roadside litter go unaddressed. The idea: build a community-driven network that prevents litter before it happens.

2025 — Growing

Building the network

Expanded outreach across Willis, built this website, and began connecting with schools, local businesses, and community organizations about the vision.

2026 — Now

Prevention-first focus

Focused on education and awareness — spreading prevention habits, building membership, and laying the groundwork for the next phase of LPN Willis.

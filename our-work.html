What We Want to Do

Our vision for a litter-free Willis

LPN Willis is a prevention-first community network. Right now we're focused on education, awareness, and connecting neighbors. Below is where we're headed — the programs and tools we're working toward to make lasting change in Willis, TX.

🗓️

Organized Cleanup Events

Regular community cleanup events at parks, roadsides, and neighborhood hotspots across Willis — open to all ages, supplies provided.

📍

Community Litter Reporting

A tool for residents to flag dumping sites and problem areas on a shared map, so the community can prioritize and respond together.

🏆

Rewards for Action

A points-based program that rewards residents for verified cleanups — redeemable for gift cards, merchandise, and local business perks.

🏫

School Partnerships

Environmental education and organized cleanup programs in Willis-area schools, building the habits that prevent litter for life.

🤝

Business Sponsorships

Local businesses funding cleanup events and supplies in exchange for community recognition — keeping participation free for everyone.

📊

Member Dashboards

Personal dashboards for members to track cleanup history, point balance, and total impact on the Willis community.

Our Story

How we got here

2024 — Founded

LPN Willis launches

Founded by a small group of Willis residents tired of seeing roadside litter go unaddressed. The idea: build a community-driven network that prevents litter before it happens.

2025 — Growing

Building the network

Expanded outreach across Willis, built this website, and began connecting with schools, local businesses, and community organizations about the vision.

2026 — Now

Prevention-first focus

Focused on education and awareness — spreading prevention habits, building membership, and laying the groundwork for Phase 2 tools and events.

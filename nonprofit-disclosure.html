Nonprofit Disclosure

Transparency about our organization and use of funds.

Organization Status

The Litter Prevention Network is organized as a nonprofit organization in the State of Texas. We operate in accordance with applicable state and federal laws governing nonprofit organizations.

Our Mission

The mission of the Litter Prevention Network is to prevent litter through education, community partnerships, youth programs, and public awareness initiatives. We work to inspire responsible habits and environmental stewardship in the communities we serve.

Use of Donations

Donations and contributions made to the Litter Prevention Network are used exclusively to fund programs, community outreach, educational materials, and operational costs directly related to our mission.

No Profit Distribution

As a nonprofit organization, the Litter Prevention Network does not distribute profits, earnings, or assets to individuals, founders, officers, or directors. All financial resources are reinvested directly into the mission.

Contact for Financial Questions

If you have questions about our nonprofit status, financials, or use of funds, please contact us at stanley@litterpreventionnetwork.org.

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to an inclusive and accessible website.

Our Commitment

The Litter Prevention Network is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe that access to information about community programs and litter prevention should be available to all members of our community.

Standards We Follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

Accessibility Features on This Site

  • Semantic HTML markup for proper document structure
  • Descriptive alt text on all images
  • Keyboard navigability throughout the site
  • Skip to main content link at the top of every page
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios for text and interactive elements
  • Labeled form inputs on all forms
  • Descriptive link text and button labels

Known Limitations

While we strive to meet accessibility standards, some content may not yet fully conform. We are actively working to identify and address any accessibility barriers on this site.

Contact Us

If you experience any accessibility barrier on this site, please contact us at stanley@litterpreventionnetwork.org. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information in an alternative format upon request.

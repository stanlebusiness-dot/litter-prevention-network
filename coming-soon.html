Phase 2 Roadmap

Future Phases

LPN is growing. These features are in development and will launch as the network expands.

Where We Are Today

Right now, LPN Willis is focused on litter prevention — educating the community, connecting neighbors, and building the habits that stop litter before it starts. The features below represent Phase 2 of our vision: a full rewards-based cleanup platform once our community foundation is in place.

Coming in Phase 2
📍

Litter Reporting

Spot a litter hotspot? Report it on an interactive community map so neighbors and volunteers know where to focus.

Phase 2
🗺️

Litter Map

A live, community-maintained map showing reported litter areas, cleaned zones, and high-priority locations across Willis.

Phase 2
📅

Organized Cleanup Events

Join scheduled neighborhood cleanups led by LPN volunteers. Browse upcoming events and RSVP directly through the site.

Phase 2
🙌

Volunteer Program

Sign up as a regular cleanup volunteer, get assigned to local routes, and track your total contribution to the community.

Phase 2
📸

Photo Verification & Points

Submit before-and-after photos to verify completed cleanups and earn points toward rewards — fully automated.

Phase 2
🏆

Rewards & Prize Portal

Earn points for every cleanup and redeem them for gas cards, gift cards, and local business perks in the prize portal.

Phase 2
📊

Member Dashboard

A personal dashboard to track your cleanup history, point balance, referrals, and your impact on the Willis community.

Phase 2

Stay in the loop

Sign up on the home page to be notified when Phase 2 launches. Your membership carries over — you'll be first in line for rewards.

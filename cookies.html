Cookie Policy

How we use cookies on this website.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide basic analytics about how visitors use a site.

How We Use Cookies

The Litter Prevention Network website uses a minimal number of cookies for the following purposes:

  • Basic site functionality (such as remembering your language preference)
  • Anonymous analytics to help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • Session management for logged-in members

What We Do Not Do

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or any technology to sell your data to advertisers or third parties. We do not build behavioral profiles based on your browsing activity.

Managing Cookies

You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some site functionality, such as staying logged in to your account.

Questions

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at stanley@litterpreventionnetwork.org.

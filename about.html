Our MissionNuestra Misión

Our mission is simple and focused: to empower people to prevent litter by taking responsibility for waste and leading a culture of environmental stewardship in their homes and communities. Nuestra misión es simple y enfocada: empoderar a las personas para prevenir la basura asumiendo responsabilidad por los residuos y liderando una cultura de responsabilidad ambiental en sus hogares y comunidades.

The first step of many. 🌿 El primer paso de muchos. 🌿

Our Story

The Litter Prevention Network was founded by people who believed that litter is not something we should simply accept as a part of everyday life.

Like many others, we grew tired of seeing trash scattered along our roads, highways, neighborhoods, parks, and public spaces. We often heard people complain about the problem, but we rarely saw efforts focused on preventing litter before it happened. Instead of accepting litter as normal, we chose to take action.

What began as conversations and brainstorming sessions soon evolved into a shared vision: creating a culture where litter prevention becomes a way of life. We believe that cleaner communities begin with personal responsibility, community pride, and a commitment to protecting the places we live, work, and play.

At the heart of our mission is the belief that lasting change begins with education. By reaching children at an early age and reinforcing positive habits throughout life, we can help create future generations that view litter prevention as a normal and expected part of being a responsible citizen.

Through educational programs, community partnerships, public awareness campaigns, volunteer initiatives, and practical litter-prevention solutions, we are working to address the root causes of litter and inspire positive action. Programs such as Trash Rangers empower young people to become leaders in environmental stewardship while helping spread our message throughout their families and communities.

We believe that everyone has a role to play. Whether it is properly containing trash, securing vehicle loads, reporting illegal dumping, picking up litter when it is safe to do so, or simply leading by example, small actions can create meaningful change when multiplied across an entire community.

The Litter Prevention Network is built on a simple idea: if we can change habits, we can change communities. If we can change communities, we can change future generations.

We are not waiting for someone else to solve the problem. We are choosing action over complaints, responsibility over excuses, and prevention over acceptance.

Together, we can create cleaner roads, cleaner neighborhoods, and a lasting culture of environmental stewardship.

Every movement begins with a decision to act.

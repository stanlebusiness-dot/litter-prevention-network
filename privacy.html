Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

What Information We Collect

When you sign up on our website, we collect the following information: your first and last name, email address, phone number (optional), zip code, and age. This information is submitted through our membership registration form.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Communicate with members about LPN programs, events, and updates
  • Send newsletters and community announcements
  • Improve our programs and outreach based on community feedback
  • Verify eligibility for age-restricted participation

We Do Not Sell Your Information

The Litter Prevention Network does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Your information is used solely to support your membership and LPN's mission.

Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing us at stanley@litterpreventionnetwork.org with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We will process your request promptly.

Cookie Usage

This site uses minimal cookies for basic analytics and site functionality. We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

Children's Privacy

The Litter Prevention Network welcomes participation from people of all ages. However, anyone under the age of 18 must have a parent or guardian complete the registration process on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the website at litterpreventionnetwork.org only. It does not apply to third-party websites, services, or platforms that may be linked from our site.

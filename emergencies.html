Emergency ResourcesRecursos de Emergencia

If you're dealing with a litter, dumping, or environmental emergency in Willis, TX — here are the right people to contact.Si estás lidiando con una emergencia de basura, vertido ilegal o ambiental en Willis, TX, aquí están las personas correctas a quienes contactar.

🚨

Life-threatening emergency? Call 911 immediately.

Fire, injury, active chemical spill, or any situation with immediate risk to life — call 911 first. Everything on this page is for non-life-threatening environmental situations.

Before You Call

Stay safe first

  1. Don't approach hazardous materials

    Chemicals, unknown liquids, or medical waste — stay at a safe distance. Document from where you stand, don't touch anything.

  2. Take a photo if it's safe to do so

    A clear photo with location context helps authorities prioritize and respond faster.

  3. Know your exact location

    Have a street address or cross-street ready before you call. For rural areas, note nearby landmarks or GPS coordinates.

Who to Call

The right agency for the situation

Different situations are handled by different agencies. Use this as a quick reference to reach the right people.

🚨

Any Life-Threatening Emergency

Fire, injury, active chemical exposure, or anyone in immediate danger.

→ Call 911
👮

Willis Police Department

Non-emergency local law enforcement — illegal dumping, suspicious activity, or abandoned vehicles on city streets.

→ (936) 856-3544  (non-emergency)
🏛️

Montgomery County Sheriff

Non-emergency situations outside city limits — illegal dumping on county roads or in unincorporated areas.

→ (936) 760-5800  (non-emergency)
🏙️

City of Willis — Code Enforcement

Illegal dumping or waste on city property, code violations, or abandoned lots accumulating trash.

→ (936) 856-1600
🛣️

TxDOT — Road Debris & Dumping

Debris, litter, or illegal dumping on state highways and TxDOT right-of-way corridors near Willis.

→ 1-800-452-5723
☣️

TCEQ — Environmental Hazards

Chemical spills, waterway contamination, hazardous waste dumping, or anything entering storm drains, ditches, or creeks.

→ 1-888-777-7798
🌊

EPA National Response Center

Major chemical releases, oil spills, or large-scale hazardous material events affecting land or waterways.

→ 1-800-424-8802
🐾

Montgomery County Animal Control

Wildlife or animals entangled in litter, or injured animals found on roads or in parks in the Willis area.

→ (936) 538-8138

When in doubt, call the right agency

These services exist to handle these situations. You don't need to clean it up yourself — just get the right people informed.

EmergenciesEmergencias

How to report hazardous litter, illegal dumping, and environmental emergencies in Willis, TX.Cómo reportar basura peligrosa, vertidos ilegales y emergencias ambientales en Willis, TX.

⚠️

Life-threatening emergency? Call 911.

This page covers environmental and litter-related emergencies. If you see a situation that puts someone's safety at immediate risk — fire, injury, active chemical spill — call 911 first. Then report it to us at (281) 935-9097.

Types of Emergencies

What counts as an environmental emergency?

Not all litter is equal. Some situations need an immediate response — from us, from the city, or from state agencies. Here's how we categorize what we see in Willis.

☣️

Hazardous Materials

Chemicals, oil drums, medical waste, or unknown liquids dumped on roadsides or in drainage areas. Do not approach or touch.

→ Call (281) 935-9097 immediately
🪣

Illegal Dumping — Large Scale

Truckloads of construction debris, appliances, or bulk waste dumped on public or private land. Often happens overnight.

→ Report via our litter map or call us
🌊

Waterway Contamination

Litter, chemicals, or runoff entering storm drains, ditches, creeks, or Lake Conroe tributaries in the Willis area.

→ Report + contact TCEQ if chemical
🔥

Litter Fire Hazard

Large accumulations of dry debris, cardboard, or waste near structures or dry vegetation during high fire-risk conditions.

→ Call 911 if active, report to us if not
🐾

Wildlife Entanglement Risk

Fishing line, netting, plastic rings, or other debris posing a hazard to local wildlife in green spaces or near water.

→ Report via litter map
🏚️

Abandoned Property Litter

Vacant lots or abandoned properties accumulating large volumes of waste attracting pests or creating neighborhood blight.

→ Report to us + contact the city
How to Report

What to do when you see an emergency

  1. Stay safe — don't approach hazardous materials

    If you see chemicals, unknown liquids, or anything that smells dangerous, stay at a distance. Document from a safe position.

  2. Take a photo if it's safe to do so

    A clear photo with the location in frame helps our team and city officials prioritize and respond faster.

  3. Report it on our litter map

    Use the "Report Litter" tool to pin the location, describe what you see, and attach your photo. Select "Emergency / Hazardous" as the type.

  4. Call us directly for urgent situations

    For anything that needs same-day attention, call (281) 935-9097. We coordinate with city public works, TxDOT, and TCEQ depending on the situation.

Our MissionNuestra Misión

Stop litter before it startsDetén la basura antes de que empiece

Litter Prevention Network is a nonprofit organization focused on preventing litter by shaping the next generation before the problem begins. La Red de Prevención de Basura es una organización sin fines de lucro enfocada en prevenir la basura formando a la próxima generación antes de que el problema comience.

By age five, many lifelong habits have already begun to form. That's why Trash Rangers focuses on reaching children as early as possible. Through educational games, stories, activities, and positive reinforcement, we make caring for our environment and communities exciting and rewarding. A los cinco años, muchos hábitos de por vida ya han comenzado a formarse. Por eso Trash Rangers se enfoca en llegar a los niños lo antes posible. A través de juegos educativos, historias, actividades y refuerzo positivo, hacemos que cuidar nuestro medio ambiente y comunidades sea emocionante y gratificante.

Every child who learns not to litter today can become an adult who naturally protects their community tomorrow. Cada niño que aprende a no tirar basura hoy puede convertirse en un adulto que proteja naturalmente su comunidad mañana.

With our community engagement program we inspire people to make a change toward a more responsible disposal of trash to prevent littering by offering a solution through affordable and convenient ways of trash containment. Con nuestro programa de participación comunitaria, inspiramos a las personas a hacer un cambio hacia una eliminación de basura más responsable para prevenir la basura, ofreciendo una solución a través de formas asequibles y convenientes de contención de basura.

Prevention over cleanup. Always.Prevención sobre limpieza. Siempre.

Our Story

The Litter Prevention Network was founded by people who believed that litter is not something we should simply accept as a part of everyday life.

Like many others, we grew tired of seeing trash scattered along our roads, highways, neighborhoods, parks, and public spaces. We often heard people complain about the problem, but we rarely saw efforts focused on preventing litter before it happened. Instead of accepting litter as normal, we chose to take action.

What began as conversations and brainstorming sessions soon evolved into a shared vision: creating a culture where litter prevention becomes a way of life. We believe that cleaner communities begin with personal responsibility, community pride, and a commitment to protecting the places we live, work, and play.

At the heart of our mission is the belief that lasting change begins with education. By reaching children at an early age and reinforcing positive habits throughout life, we can help create future generations that view litter prevention as a normal and expected part of being a responsible citizen.

Through educational programs, community partnerships, public awareness campaigns, volunteer initiatives, and practical litter-prevention solutions, we are working to address the root causes of litter and inspire positive action. Programs such as Trash Rangers empower young people to become leaders in environmental stewardship while helping spread our message throughout their families and communities.

We believe that everyone has a role to play. Whether it is properly containing trash, securing vehicle loads, reporting illegal dumping, picking up litter when it is safe to do so, or simply leading by example, small actions can create meaningful change when multiplied across an entire community.

The Litter Prevention Network is built on a simple idea: if we can change habits, we can change communities. If we can change communities, we can change future generations.

We are not waiting for someone else to solve the problem. We are choosing action over complaints, responsibility over excuses, and prevention over acceptance.

Together, we can create cleaner roads, cleaner neighborhoods, and a lasting culture of environmental stewardship.

Every movement begins with a decision to act.

How You Can HelpCómo Puedes Ayudar

What You Can DoLo Que Puedes Hacer

You don't have to wait for an event or sign up for a program to make a difference. These simple everyday habits stop litter before it starts.No tienes que esperar un evento ni inscribirte en un programa para marcar la diferencia. Estos simples hábitos cotidianos detienen la basura antes de que comience.

🚗

Keep trash in your vehicleMantén la basura en tu vehículo

Keep all trash inside your vehicle until it can be properly disposed of in a trash can. Lead by example.Mantén toda la basura dentro de tu vehículo hasta que pueda desecharse correctamente en un contenedor. Da el ejemplo.

🚛

Nothing loose in the truck bedNada suelto en la caja

Do not place loose trash in the bed of your pickup truck. Wind can carry it onto our roads where it becomes litter.No coloques basura suelta en la caja de tu camioneta. El viento puede llevarla a nuestras carreteras y convertirla en basura.

🗑️

Use a vehicle trash containerUsa un contenedor de basura para tu vehículo

Keep trash contained inside your vehicle. Use a vehicle trash container or bag to prevent trash from escaping when doors or windows are opened.Mantén la basura contenida dentro de tu vehículo. Usa un contenedor o bolsa para evitar que la basura escape cuando se abren puertas o ventanas.

⛓️

Secure every loadAsegura cada carga

Properly secure cargo, materials, and debris to prevent them from falling onto roads and highways on every trip.Asegura adecuadamente la carga, materiales y escombros para evitar que caigan en carreteras y autopistas en cada viaje.

🤏

Pick up one pieceRecoge una pieza

Every piece of litter removed is one less piece that can spread into our communities, roads, waterways, and natural areas. You don't have to be the person who dropped it to be the person who makes a difference.Cada pieza de basura recogida es una pieza menos que puede extenderse en nuestras comunidades, carreteras y áreas naturales. No tienes que ser quien la tiró para ser quien hace la diferencia.

🪧

Remove temporary signsRetira los letreros temporales

Remove temporary signs when they are no longer needed. Old yard signs and event signs can become litter if left behind or blown away.Retira los letreros temporales cuando ya no sean necesarios. Los letreros viejos de jardín o eventos pueden convertirse en basura si se dejan atrás o el viento los lleva.

🍬

Dispose of gum properlyDesecha el chicle correctamente

Wrap chewing gum in paper and place it in a trash can rather than discarding it on the ground.Envuelve el chicle en papel y colócalo en un bote de basura en lugar de desecharlo en el suelo.

🎒

Secure bags and backpacksAsegura bolsas y mochilas

Store loose trash securely in purses, backpacks, and bags to prevent it from accidentally falling out.Almacena la basura suelta de forma segura en bolsos, mochilas y bolsas para evitar que caiga accidentalmente.

🎭

Clean up at eventsLimpia en los eventos

Take your trash with you when attending sporting events, concerts, theaters, parks, and other public gatherings. Help keep shared spaces clean by disposing of waste in designated trash containers.Lleva tu basura contigo cuando asistas a eventos deportivos, conciertos, teatros, parques y otras reuniones públicas. Ayuda a mantener limpios los espacios compartidos.

🍾

Bottle it, don't litter itEmbotéllalo, no lo tires

If you smoke, take a small glass bottle with a little water in your car to use as a portable ashtray for cigarette butts. Cigarette butts are one of the most commonly littered items found along roadsides and waterways. A single butt can take years to break down and may release harmful chemicals into the environment.Si fumas, lleva una pequeña botella de vidrio con un poco de agua en tu auto para usarla como cenicero portátil. Las colillas son uno de los artículos más comúnmente tirados a lo largo de carreteras y vías fluviales.

📸

Share your impactComparte tu impacto

If you see an area with litter and can safely help, take a few minutes to clean it up. Take before-and-after photos and submit them through our website or share on social media. Your efforts may be featured on our website, newsletters, or community outreach materials.Si ves un área con basura y puedes ayudar de forma segura, tómate unos minutos para limpiarla. Toma fotos antes y después y compártelas en redes sociales o envíalas a través de nuestro sitio web.

📍

Report problems safelyReporta problemas con seguridad

If you see illegal dumping, large amounts of litter, or recurring problem areas, safely document the location and report it to the appropriate authority.Si ves vertido ilegal, grandes cantidades de basura o áreas problemáticas recurrentes, documenta la ubicación de forma segura y repórtala a la autoridad correspondiente.

Getting InvolvedCómo Participar

At this time, the Litter Prevention Network does not have any organized cleanup events scheduled. However, we may host community cleanup events in the future. In the meantime, we encourage individuals, families, schools, businesses, and community groups to organize their own cleanup activities when it is safe to do so.

If you plan to participate in a cleanup, consider bringing:

  • Work gloves or litter pickup tools
  • Trash bags
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Comfortable clothing appropriate for the weather
  • Sunscreen, a hat, and other sun protection
  • Drinking water to stay hydrated
  • A fully charged phone to take before-and-after photos and share your impact

Most importantly, always put safety first. Avoid hazardous materials, sharp objects, busy roadways, private property, and any area that may present a risk. Children should always be supervised by a responsible adult. Remember: no amount of litter is worth risking your safety. If an area appears unsafe, report the issue to the appropriate authority rather than attempting to clean it yourself.

  • Keep all trash inside your vehicle until it can be properly disposed of
  • Do not place loose trash in the bed of your pickup truck — wind can carry it onto roads
  • Use a vehicle trash container or bag to keep trash contained
  • Secure every load on every trip using cargo nets, tarps, or straps
  • Remove temporary signs when they are no longer needed
  • Dispose of chewing gum properly — wrap it and place it in a trash can
  • Take your trash with you when attending events, parks, and public gatherings
  • If you smoke, use a small bottle with water as a portable ashtray for cigarette butts — "Bottle it, don't litter it"
  • Lead by example every day

Why It MattersPor Qué Importa

Litter does more than create an eyesore. Wind and rain carry litter into storm drains, creeks, rivers, lakes, and eventually oceans. Wildlife can become entangled in plastic debris or mistake litter for food. Chemicals from discarded waste can contaminate soil and water. Litter creates safety hazards — debris that falls from vehicles can cause accidents and damage property. Cities and counties spend millions of dollars each year cleaning litter from roadsides, parks, and public spaces. The good news is that litter is one of the most preventable environmental problems. Small actions taken by many people can create a tremendous impact.
